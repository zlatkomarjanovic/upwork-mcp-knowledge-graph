# Upwork demand insights

- WordPress and Elementor remain the highest-volume title-match lane in the last 2 hours (redesigns, small fixes, agency multi-site builds).
- Full stack and Next.js/React posts skew toward production SaaS and AI-assisted delivery (Claude Code/Cursor mentioned in several listings).
- Webflow demand is active on completion, Figma-to-Webflow, and migration off WordPress/Shopify.
- GoHighLevel shows fresh on-call specialist, forensic audit, and CRM automation posts (often paired with n8n/voice AI).
- Vibe coding / Lovable / Bolt.new volume is real but noisier; many posts are app/game adjacent, not classic web dev.
- Maintenance retainers appear steadily on WordPress; fewer pure Webflow maintenance title matches than WordPress.
